## PRIORITY GUIDE FOR AI AGENTS

### Must-Do Problems (Marked V.IMP in original sheet)
- Array: Kadane's Algo, Minimise max difference between heights
- LinkedList: Reverse in groups, Flattening, Merge Sort for LL
- Stacks & Queues: LRU Cache
- BST: Largest BST in Binary Tree, Merge two BSTs
- Binary Trees: Check duplicate subtrees

### Topic Priority Order for Beginners
1. Array → 2. String → 3. LinkedList → 4. Stacks & Queues → 5. Binary Trees → 6. BST → 7. Searching & Sorting → 8. Greedy → 9. Heap → 10. Graph → 11. BackTracking → 12. DP → 13. Trie → 14. Matrix → 15. Bit Manipulation

### Interview Frequency (High to Low)
- **Very High:** Array, DP, Graph, String, LinkedList
- **High:** Trees, BST, Stacks & Queues
- **Medium:** Greedy, Backtracking, Heap, Searching & Sorting
- **Lower:** Trie, Matrix, Bit Manipulation
